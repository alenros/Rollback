# Rollback Colors: pass & replace

*A variant of Colors for playtesting a gentler way to work on your screen. 2–5 players.*

**All the Colors rules apply, picking up included, with one new move:**

## Replacing
- Instead of beating, passing, or picking up, you may **swap one of your dice for one die in the play on the table**. Put your die into the play, take the table die behind your screen, and **reroll the die you took**. The die you put in keeps its value.
- What's on the table must still be a **legal play** afterwards, but it may change: a pair of 5s can become the run 5-6, and a one-color play can become mixed (or the other way round).
- The table play stays with the player who made it.
- Replacing **counts as a pass** for ending the trick. You don't get the pass rerolls, and the number of dice behind your screen stays the same.

### Bomb shapes
A swap may leave a **same-color 5-run** or a **rainbow 4-set** on the table. It has **no bomb effect**: it doesn't clear the table or end the trick, and it is beaten like the plain run or set it is. So a red 3-4-5-6-7 on the table is beaten by a one-color run of 6 or by any bomb, and a rainbow set of 6s by a set of five, a set of four higher, or any bomb.

## Why
Picking up always grows your screen. Replacing lets you trade a die you don't want for a fresh roll without growing your screen, and it can weaken the play on the table, or reshape it, before your next turn.
