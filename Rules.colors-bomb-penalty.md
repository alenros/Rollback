# Rollback Colors: bomb penalty

*A variant of Colors for playtesting whether bombs are too strong. 2–5 players.*

**All the Colors rules apply, with one change:**

## Bombs cost a die
- A bomb still beats any single, set, or run, whatever its color. It still ends the trick at once, and you still lead the next trick.
- But a bomb now **costs you**: take back **one of the dice you bombed**, your choice, behind your screen, and **reroll it**. The other bombed dice and the bomb itself leave the round.
- If only one die was bombed, you take that one.
- **Going out comes first.** If the bomb uses the last dice behind your screen, you go out and take nothing back.

## Why
A bomb is otherwise the best move in the game: 5 (or 4) dice out, nothing back, and you keep the lead. The penalty makes it a strong play with a price, like a trump that ends the trick but slows you down.
